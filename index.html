03 / The fundamentals

What automation and web development mean.

Two quiet disciplines do most of the work behind a well-run business online. Here is what each one means — and why they belong together.

W Web development

What is web development?

The design and build of your website — its pages, words, visuals and the code underneath. It is how customers find you, understand what you do and decide whether to get in touch.

Good web development makes the site a working part of the business: clear, quick to load, easy to update and built to earn trust.

  • Websites
  • Web apps
  • UI & UX
A Automation

What is automation?

Automation is software doing the repetitive parts on its own — taking an enquiry, replying on WhatsApp, updating a sheet, reminding a customer or handing work to the right person.

It is the system behind the service: predictable, quiet and always on, so your team spends less time copying and more time helping.

  • Workflows
  • WhatsApp
  • AI systems

Used together, a website does more than look good: every form, message and reminder lands where it should — with no enquiry lost between the two.
